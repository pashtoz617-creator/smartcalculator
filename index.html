What is Smart Calculator?

A free, simple Android app in Pashto, Dari and English for everyday needs.

Features

  • 🧮 Calculator – fast, with large keys
  • 🔄 Unit converter – length, weight (including seer and kg), temperature
  • 🎂 Age calculator
  • 🏷️ Discount calculator
  • 📅 Calendar converter – Gregorian, Shamsi (Solar Hijri) and Hijri (lunar)
  • 🌙 Light and dark mode

How to install

  1. Tap the download button above.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If asked, allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
  4. Tap Install, then open the app.

If Play Protect shows a warning, choose "More details → Install anyway". The app does not ask for your personal information.
